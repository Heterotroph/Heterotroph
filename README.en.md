### I'm building ISKRA AI so whole companies can work with AI agents as one team

We started with marketing agents that actually do the work instead of handing out advice. Now we're building an enterprise harness around them, where skills, data, and know-how belong to the company. They have no business living on employees' laptops.

I've been in software for 15+ years. I built payment kiosk UIs at QIWI, was one of the UI developers behind the **Nintendo Switch port of The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt** at Saber Interactive, shipped web products from scratch, and ran the entire IT function at Zvuk Business as CTO.

**An enterprise AI harness for large companies.** One multi-user, multi-tenant workspace instead of a sprawl of personal Claude Code and Codex setups.

- Agents push big tasks toward target metrics over the long haul, without a human nudge at every step.
